Mastermind Council — seven thinkers debate your idea

Bring a business, a project or a decision you keep turning over. A council of historical thinkers debates it among themselves for a few rounds, then returns a verdict and one next action. You can answer back and the debate continues.

The roster

  • Socrates — asks the question you are avoiding
  • Marcus Aurelius — what is actually in your control
  • Seneca — the cost in time and money
  • Buddha — the attachment driving the plan
  • Confucius — duty, relationships, who carries the work
  • Benjamin Franklin — the cheap experiment you can run this week
  • Maria Montessori — what the user learns by doing

One shared question every day, the same for everyone, with a shareable result card that shows how your council split and never your own text.

A fictional interpretation of historical thinkers, generated by AI. Not the words or opinions of the real people, and not medical, legal or financial advice.
