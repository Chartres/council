Mastermind Council — ten thinkers debate your idea

Bring a business, a project or a decision you keep turning over. Pick up to four historical thinkers; a facilitator runs the conversation, you steer it, and it ends in one decision and one next action you keep in a journal.

The Classics roster

  • Socrates — asks the question you are avoiding
  • Marcus Aurelius — what is actually in your control
  • Seneca — the cost in time and money
  • Buddha — the attachment driving the plan
  • Confucius — duty, relationships, who carries the work
  • Benjamin Franklin — the cheap experiment you can run this week
  • Maria Montessori — what the user learns by doing

The Business roster seats Socrates with three simulations inspired by Peter Drucker, Steve Jobs and Andy Grove: ten advisers in all.
