Mastermind Council

How a council session works

Pick up to four advisers, say what you are deciding, talk it through with them and a neutral facilitator, and keep the decision in a journal with one next action.

Pick the room

Choose a roster, Classics or Business, and seat up to four advisers. A neutral facilitator is always there and keeps the session moving.

Say what you are deciding

One screen of short questions: the goal, the situation, what you have tried. Paste text, drop a file, ask your own AI to fill it in, or say it aloud. Every question can be skipped.

Talk it through

The advisers answer in turn and the facilitator puts one question on the floor. You reply, or steer: go back to an earlier point, let two advisers explore it between them, ask for disagreement, ask for something concrete, or wrap up.

Keep the decision

When the group lands somewhere, it proposes a decision with its reasoning, assumptions and one next action. You edit it and accept it into your journal.

Commit and follow up

Give the next action a due date as a commitment and, if you are signed in, an email reminder on that day. Next time, the session opens with "Last time…" and asks how it went.

Talk mode

A spoken conversation instead of typing: the mic stays open, each adviser answers in a distinct synthetic voice, and you can cut in.

From your own AI

The same council works inside Claude or ChatGPT as a connector: https://llm.dravec.org/mcp, signed in by email.

Quotes are checked

The Classics advisers are written from public-domain translations, and any line an adviser puts in quotation marks is checked against those texts first. See the advisers and what the classics wrote.
