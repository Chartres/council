What this adviser draws on

Autobiography and The Way to Wealth (1758), via Project Gutenberg. In a session the adviser is an AI simulation written from this text. When it quotes, the quote is checked against the text before you see it; a line that cannot be found is shown as paraphrase, not in quotation marks. 52 lines are in its verified bank.
