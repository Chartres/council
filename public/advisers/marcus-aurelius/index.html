Strips the idea down to what is actually in your control.

What this adviser draws on

Meditations (The Thoughts of the Emperor Marcus Aurelius Antoninus), trans. George Long (1862), via Wikisource. In a session the adviser is an AI simulation written from this text. When it quotes, the quote is checked against the text before you see it; a line that cannot be found is shown as paraphrase, not in quotation marks. 52 lines are in its verified bank.
