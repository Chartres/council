How the quotes are checked

Each thinker has a bank of lines taken from one public-domain translation: Gummere's Seneca, George Long's Marcus Aurelius, Jowett's Plato, Legge's Analects, Müller's Dhammapada with Bhikkhu Sujato's suttas, Anne E. George's Montessori, and Franklin's own Autobiography and Way to Wealth. A script downloaded each edition and checked that every line is a contiguous match in the named letter, book, chapter or verse. In the app, a quotation an adviser makes is checked against the source passages before it is shown; one that cannot be found is removed before you see it.
