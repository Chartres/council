What the classics wrote about power

Power in two senses: what is in your control (Marcus Aurelius and Seneca keep returning to it), and the power one person holds over another (Montessori and Confucius on teachers and rulers).

Every line below appears word for word in the public-domain translation named under it; a script checked each one against the text of that edition. The link opens the letter, book or edition it came from.
